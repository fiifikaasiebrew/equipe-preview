Delivery and returns

Last updated 24 November 2026

Delivery in Accra

We deliver to your door in Greater Accra. The delivery fee, if any, is shown in your bag before you pay. We message you on WhatsApp to agree a time.

Delivery outside Accra

We send parcels by bus or courier across Ghana. We confirm the route, the fee and the pickup point with you on WhatsApp before sending.

Pickup in Accra

Choose pickup at checkout and we message you the pickup point and time. Please bring your order number.

How long it takes

We pack paid orders quickly and message you when they are on the way. Delivery times depend on the area and the courier, and we tell you when we confirm.

Checking your parcel

Please look over your piece when it arrives. If something is wrong, tell us as soon as you can.

Returns and problems

Because every piece is one of a kind, we do not do returns for a change of mind. If a piece arrives that is not as described (a different size, damage we did not mention, or the wrong item), message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of receiving it with your order number and clear photos. We will agree a fix with you: an exchange for another piece, or a refund to the way you paid.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the Mobile Money number or card you paid with. Payment providers can take a few days to show it.
