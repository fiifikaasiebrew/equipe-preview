Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

équipe clothes store ("équipe", "we", "us") sells hand-picked clothes from Accra, Ghana. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number (WhatsApp), delivery area, address and landmark, optional email and notes, and what you ordered.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp: your phone number and the messages you send.
  • On your device: your bag, saved items and recently viewed items are kept in your own browser (see our Cookies page). They are not sent to us until you place an order.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your card number, Mobile Money PIN or any payment secret. Payments are handled by Paystack on their own secure page. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your information.

Why we use it

  • To take, confirm, pack and deliver your order.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp about your order, size questions and pickup or delivery.
  • To send a receipt if you gave an email.
  • To keep records of sales as the law requires, and to prevent fraud.

Who we share it with

  • Paystack, to process card and Mobile Money payments.
  • Delivery riders, bus or courier companies, only the name, phone number and address needed to deliver your parcel.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you chat with us there.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, returns and legal obligations. After that they are deleted or made anonymous.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Message us on WhatsApp (see the Contact page) and we will answer within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Security

Staff areas are protected by a password and a sign-in that expires. We keep access to customer details limited to the people who need it.

Children

The shop is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please order with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
