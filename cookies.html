Cookies and storage

Last updated 24 November 2026

We keep this simple: the website only uses what it needs to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics that follow you around.

What is stored on your device

NameBrowser storage (not a cookie): your bag, your saved items, the pieces you looked at recently, and your cookie notice choice. It stays on your device and is not sent to us until you place an order. You can clear it in your browser settings.
admStaff sign-in cookie. Set only when staff sign in to the staff area. It expires after 12 hours or when they log out. Shoppers never get this cookie.

Third parties

When you choose to pay, you go to Paystack, which has its own cookies and policy. When you tap the WhatsApp button you go to WhatsApp, which has its own policy.

Your choices

You can clear or block browser storage and cookies in your browser settings. If you do, your bag and saved items will not be remembered between visits.
